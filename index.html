FAQs

Good questions to ask first.

Can’t see yours? Call +44 7446 950 389 and ask us directly.

Are you a lender?–

No. Posfin Capital Limited is an authorised credit broker and not a lender. We work with a panel of lenders whose particulars will be supplied upon request to find a potentially suitable option.

What is bridging finance used for?+

Short-term finance while you buy, sell, refinance or wait for longer-term funding: for example a property purchase, an auction purchase, a chain break, refurbishment works, refinancing an existing lender or a development exit.

Does the fact find affect my credit?+

No. It is a fact-finding form only. Nothing is sent to a lender and no credit search is made.

Is my loan regulated by the FCA?+

It depends on the loan. Loans for personal, domestic or residential purposes are typically regulated. Business-purpose loans, for example through an SPV or limited company, are often not. If you are unsure, contact us and we will confirm before you proceed.

How do you get paid?+

We will receive commission from lenders. Different lenders pay different amounts depending on different commission models. For transparency, we work with the following commission models where applicable: fixed fee, fixed rate of commission, percentage of the amount you borrow and rate for risk, based on the risk profile of the business. Further details of the commission model, calculation and amount will be disclosed to you throughout your customer journey.

Who will I actually speak to?+

Byron or Chris handles your case from initial call to drawdown. Our AI assistants help with updates and paperwork, and are always labelled as AI.
