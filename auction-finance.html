Auction Finance

Short-term bridging to complete an auction purchase, built around the typical 28-day timetable. Subject to lender terms and status.

Typically up to 75%of the purchase price, or the lender’s valuation if lower
Typically 12 monthstypical term, assessed case by case
Interest can be retainedtypically no monthly payments during the term
  • Completion is aimed at the usual 28-day auction deadline, subject to valuation, legal work, and lender terms and status.
  • Typically up to 75% of the purchase price, using the purchased property as security. The net amount you receive is usually lower than the gross loan once fees and any retained interest are deducted.
  • A higher overall facility may be possible by using additional security, or equity in a property you already own, subject to lender terms and status. Think carefully before securing other debts against your home.
  • Interest can typically be retained, so there are usually no monthly payments during the term. Retained interest is added to the loan and is repayable with the balance.
  • Typical exits are a refinance onto a buy-to-let mortgage, or a refurbishment and sale. The exit is assessed case by case and is subject to lender terms.
