Posfin Capital Limited is an authorised credit broker and not a lender. We work with a panel of lenders whose particulars will be supplied upon request to find a potentially suitable option.

We will receive commission from lenders. Different lenders pay different amounts depending on different commission models. For transparency, we work with the following commission models where applicable: fixed fee, fixed rate of commission, percentage of the amount you borrow and rate for risk, based on the risk profile of the business. Further details of the commission model, calculation and amount will be disclosed to you throughout your customer journey.
