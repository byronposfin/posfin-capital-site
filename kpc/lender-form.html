How the 3-month schedule reconciles

At 3 months the published total is round(1.288 × net + £134). That matches pricing-guide.html from £26,000 (£33,622) through £100,000 (£128,934). The 1.288 slope is the KPC sub-total identity: 12% broker on net, plus 2.50% per month for 3 months charged as retained interest and again as a facility fee equal to that interest minus a fixed plug. The +£134 intercept is 0.15 × £893.33. Lines round to the pound and the facility-fee line holds any residual so they sum to the published total.

Each month after month 3 adds round(2.50% × net) once. Broker, facility fee and the £893 plug stay at the 3-month calibration. The £1,200 Ackroyds + £200 title insurance budget is £0 upfront and is not added on top of the published total. step5b uses net × 1.195 + £1,200, and calculator.js with £1,200 legals produces 1.288 × net + £180. Neither of those is the pricing-guide figure. The workbook model is the comparison: opening balance compounds at 2.50% pm, with 3.95% pm default stress for +3 and +6 months and a 5.0% pm post-term comparison.

Hard-stop triage

    KPC lender mandate profile
    • Unregulated UK residential micro-bridging only. Owner-occupied main residence goes to the Posfin regulated panel.
    • Net release £26,000–£100,000. Sweet spot about £50,000 (£40,000–£65,000).
    • Existing debt at or under 40% LTV. Above 40% is a hard decline on its own.
    • Day-1 combined LTV and gross/exit combined LTV at or under 50%. Above 50% on either measure is a hard decline.
    • Term 3–12 months. 3 months is the typical quick turn. Under 3 or over 12 is a hard decline.
    • AVM must be completed and verified before drawdown. A missing AVM blocks drawdown even when every other rule passes.
    • Settlement rails for drawdown and capital calls are Faster Payments (same-day, typically seconds) or CHAPS (same-day, fee applies, for larger sums). BACS is a hard block.
    • Bridge-to-bridge, sale, term refinance or dev exit needs a short evidence note. Title flags (charging orders, restrictions, missing consent, lease under 70 years) go to Ackroyds Legal and do not silently clear a hard stop.
    • Standard rolled rate 2.50% per month. Default stress 3.95% per month. Post-term comparison 5.0% per month. Arrangement fee assumption 2%, editable. Legals £1,200 Ackroyds + £200 title insurance, £0 upfront.
